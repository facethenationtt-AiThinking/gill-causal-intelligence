# Gill Causal Intelligence (GCI)

Research by Ricardo Gill on evidence-bounded retrodiction (EBR), outcome-first reasoning, and the translation of Gill reasoning into AI functions and implementation specifications. A Posse ad Esse: from possibility to reality.

## Research collection

The prepared sanitised public research edition dated 10 October 2026 contains three parts:

1. Gill reasoning and principles.
2. Gill Reasoning as AI Thinking.
3. Gill Reasoning Implemented in AI.

Read [the collection](Gill_Public_Research_Collection_v1.0.md), [the PDF](Gill_Public_Research_Collection_v1.0.pdf), or [the editable Word document](Gill_Public_Research_Collection_v1.0.docx).

This repository is public and available for reading, review and AI research. It contains Gill reasonings, EBR, AI thinking specifications and theories. Consciousness-continuity hypotheses appear as research topics, separate from personal legacy administration.

Personal legacy continuation, heir succession instructions, private GSZ packages, family records, financial allocations, credentials and private instructions are maintained separately and are not part of this repository. Repository collaborator access does not establish inheritance or ownership succession.

## Review status

These documents present proposed frameworks, implementation specifications and hypotheses. Uploading them does not establish scientific validation, peer review or machine-checked proofs. The full EBR-N manuscript remains a recovery task. Empirical claims and physical or consciousness hypotheses require their own evidence and discriminating tests.
